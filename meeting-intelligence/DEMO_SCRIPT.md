**[Pause]**

"We thought: what if meetings could actually... *remember*?"

### THE SOLUTION (10 seconds) - Person 1

**[Gesture to screen]**

"Meet NEXUS - the first meeting assistant with **persistent memory**. Let me show you."

---

## Handling Questions (Prepare for these)

### "How is this different from Otter.ai?"
**Answer:** "Otter transcribes single meetings in isolation. NEXUS connects meetings over time. It's the difference between a notebook and a second brain."

### "Does this work with Zoom/Google Meet?"
**Answer:** "The current demo uses transcripts, perfect for hackathon speed. Phase 2 adds real-time integration - we prioritized the AI intelligence first, because that's the hard part."

### "What's your business model?"
**Answer:** "SaaS - $10/user/month for unlimited meetings with persistent memory. Enterprise tier for compliance features. The Claude API costs are minimal compared to the value."

### "How do you handle privacy/security?"
**Answer:** "All meeting data stays in your infrastructure. We can deploy on-premise for sensitive industries. Claude API has enterprise-grade security and doesn't train on your data."

### "Can it integrate with Jira/Slack?"
**Answer:** "Absolutely - that's Phase 2. Action items can auto-create Jira tickets, Slack notifications can go out immediately. The foundation is here."

### "What if there's no past meetings?"
**Answer:** "It still provides intelligent analysis. But the real magic happens over time - each meeting adds to the memory. Week 1 is good, Week 10 is incredible."
